1. About this policy

Arrows Puzzle is a puzzle game for Android published by HyRB1k Games (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game keeps, where it is kept and how you can contact us about it.

The game does not need an account or sign-in. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no player analytics and no social-login features, and we do not run a cloud-save service or any server for the game.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves your puzzle progress and your settings on your device so it can restore them the next time you play. The settings include language, appearance, sound effects, volume, haptics and frame rate.

The game itself does not send this information to us or to anyone else.

3. Network access and Unity diagnostics

The Android version of the game does not request Android’s Internet permission, so the game app is not allowed to open network connections. In our own testing of version 1.0 on an Android phone, covering launch, play, changing settings and restarting, we observed no network traffic from the game.

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity can optionally collect engine diagnostic and performance information; we have turned this diagnostic collection and Unity analytics submission off in the released game. The game uses fonts bundled with the app rather than downloading them.

This describes the game app itself. Android and other services on your device, such as app stores, system backups, your browser or your email app, work independently of the game and handle information under their own settings and policies.

Some earlier pre-release test builds had Unity diagnostics turned on. If you installed such a test build and have a question about it, contact us at the address below. More about Unity’s handling of player data: Unity’s player privacy policy.

4. Android backups and device transfer

Depending on your device, Android version and settings, Android may include the game’s saved progress and settings in your device backup (for example, your Google account backup) or copy them when you move to a new device. This is done by Android and your chosen backup service, not by us, and we cannot access those backups.

You can manage backups in your device’s settings. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage does not by itself remove copies already stored in a backup.

5. Contacting us

If you email us at hyrb1kgames@gmail.com, we receive your email address, your message and anything else you choose to include. We use this only to answer and resolve your request.

We keep support correspondence only for as long as needed to respond to and resolve your request, unless we need to keep it longer to meet obligations that apply to us. Your email provider and ours also process messages under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Retention and deletion

Game data on your device. Your progress and settings stay on your device until you delete them. You can remove them at any time by clearing the game’s storage in Android’s app settings for Arrows Puzzle (the exact menu names vary by device) or by uninstalling the game. Clearing storage resets your progress and preferences.

Backups. Copies held in your Android backup are controlled by your backup service and device settings, as described above.

Requests to us. You can ask us to delete your support correspondence, or ask any other privacy question, by emailing hyrb1kgames@gmail.com. We will review each request and respond. Because the game has no accounts, we may ask for details that help us find the information your request refers to.

7. This policy page and external links

This page contains no analytics, advertising, forms, scripts, cookies or embedded third-party content. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

When you open this policy from the game, it opens in your web browser. Your browser and any sites you visit handle information under their own policies.

8. Security

The game does not ask for or store passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information. Saved progress and settings are kept in the game’s private storage on your device.

9. Changes to this policy

If the way the game handles information changes, for example if we add new features or services, we will update this policy and its effective date on this page.
